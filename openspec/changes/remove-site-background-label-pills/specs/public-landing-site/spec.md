## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Unlabeled hero background
The landing page hero background SHALL avoid floating text label pills over the constellation animation.

#### Scenario: Hero background renders
- **WHEN** the visitor opens the landing page
- **THEN** the hero background shows the constellation visual without background text pills such as feature cluster, ranked finding, or agent handoff labels

#### Scenario: Workflow stages render
- **WHEN** the visitor views the hero call-to-action area
- **THEN** the visible workflow stage pills for Scan, Map, Rank, and Handoff remain available below the hero copy
