## Why

The landing page hero background includes floating text pills over the constellation animation. They compete with the main headline and make the background feel busier than intended.

## What Changes

- Remove the floating constellation label pills from the hero background.
- Keep the animated constellation dots, halos, lines, and workflow stage pills below the hero copy.
- Remove unused CSS and JavaScript tied only to those background labels.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `public-landing-site`: Simplifies the hero background visual treatment.

## Impact

- Updates static site files under `site/`.
- No CLI, provider, package release, or deployment workflow behavior changes.
